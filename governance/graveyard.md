# Graveyard

Rules that were retired. No tool, hook or agent loads this file, and that is the point: the
history stays diffable and costs nothing per session.

Bury a rule here when it is no longer true, its subject was retired, or it never fired. Keep one
line per rule: the rule id, the date, and why it went. A fact about the past ("we stopped using
tool X") belongs here, not in the always-loaded file.
